About Tonga

Tonga is a beautiful island kingdom located in the South Pacific Ocean. It is made up of many islands and is known for its friendly people, rich culture, beautiful beaches, and warm tropical climate.

Tonga is the only remaining Polynesian kingdom. Its capital city is Nuku'alofa, which is located on the main island of Tongatapu.

The people of Tonga are proud of their traditions, language, family values, music, dancing, and strong connection to the ocean.
